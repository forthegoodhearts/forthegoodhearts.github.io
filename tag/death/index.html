Isn’t the PRESENT TIME the most important Time?

Let\’s go forward in time. Mark a day on your calendar and plan an event. Mark it and watch that the day come by and leave. Mark it for a month, it will come by and leave. Mark a day 1 year from now, it will come by and leave. Mark it for 4 years (such as the World Cup). You might think it is distant, but it will definitely come and you will watch it pass by, just as it did before, right, if you have a obsession of sports. Then, Mark a day even after 10 years and you will watch it pass by – if you live until then. Then what?
