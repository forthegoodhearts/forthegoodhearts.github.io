If you are a \’Believer\’ in a CREATOR, What\’s your ENORMOUS BRAIN created for?

Bismillah! Al Hamdulillah!
Wa AsSalatu AsSalamu A’ala Rasulallah!

Audience Classification: 

Be SURE that you are SOMEHOW a \’BELIEVER\’, to the least degree.

Often, you will find that a THOUGHTFUL person has a philosophy by which he runs his life. This write-up is for all of those people out there:

By whatever philosophy you live your life, if there is a Creator in your mind to the least degree, then I will address you as a \’believer\’ in this article. You may belong to ANY RELIGION or ANY SECTS of any religion, or you may not belong to any religion (if that\’s what you think about the ideas you have), but somehow you believe that there is a power, there is a SUPREME BEING, then this is for you.

Furthermore, if you are a FUNDAMENTAL \’believer\’ of your \’religion\’ or IF you are a RATIONAL \’Believer\’ of your \’religion\’ or IF you are a MODERN \’believer\’ of your \’religion\’ or if you just a \’believer\’ By BORN, whatever level of a religious person you are, then this is for you and these statements apply to you and therefore, this is directed towards you. If you cannot relate to these statements that you just read, then respectfully speaking, there is no need for you to continue reading further, and you may stop here.

The Proposal:

Alright, so you\’re certain that you have been created and that a creator exists somewhere out there? If that’s the case, and if you believe you are a CREATION, then every part of your body – your hands, legs, eyes, heart – has been created by the Creator, right? To a believer, this point must be engraved in their mind. It must not be weak or unstable. Otherwise, the point I\’m about to make will not make any sense.

Are you a THOUGHTFUL person?

First of all, you must be a thoughtful person, willing to have an open mind and think about the world around you. As a matter of fact, most human beings are more or less thoughtful, but the only thing is that not everybody is thoughtful in everything!  So, do you think you are one of the thoughtful people? As being thoughtful is the first criterion to understand the point I am going to make. Therefore, I am emphasising this to bring your attention and increase your thoughtfulness in this ONE point!

Is your BODY MADE by your CREATOR?

Do you believe that \’God\’ made you? Or do you believe that a supreme being made you, the same supreme being who kept everything in the universe in balance? Did you really think about it?

I am asking again, do you believe that \’God\’ has put you in your mother\’s womb? And then \’GOD\’ has made you a baby, and then a child, a boy or a girl, a teenager and then a man or a woman? Is that \’God\’ who brought you up, really? Is that \’God\’ who gave you these eyes, now you are reading with? This brain that you are thinking of now? Did \’God\’ give you these things?

Look at your skin, your finger, your nails, do you believe \’God\’ has made them? Give it a thought, just look at your body and give it a thought for a minute or so, do not let any other thought come and distract you from it. Think, is that \’GOD\’ who made you in this shape? Touch your body, touch your hand, touch your feet, look at them, you own them now, right? Do you? These are your part of existence, these are what makes you who you are, these belong to you.
But are you the owner of these?
Are you the creator of these?
Have you created your body parts?
Or are these things created by \’God\’?
If \’God\’ created them, who owns them? You or \’God\’, You or your Creator?

Why Your CREATOR MADE YOU?

If \’God\’ has a purpose of creating you, then all these organs of yours were created collectively for that purpose, isn\’t it?
You see, logically, everyone can understand that if someone creates something, he has a purpose for doing it, right? So, without even going to the religious books and religious ideas of \’God\’s purpose of creation of human beings, logically,y anyone can conclude that there is a purpose!

But even all the religions say that \’God\’ has a purpose of creating human beings, a believer, even who is the most unattached person with his religion, would say mockingly that \’God\’ has created us for his \”PLEASURE\”. Truly, that \’pleasure\’ is what is termed especially in the religious books as worshipping \’God\’. We can put the references from all the different religious books later. But for now, we are here and we understand that we are a creation and we were created for a purpose.

Why do you create something?

Now, imagine you create something, say a robot. In fact, scientists have already created robots. What are the components of a robot? Same as human beings, hands, legs, face, eyes, etc., at least we can see the external organs. Just also think of electronic dolls that kids play with today, they are also a kind of robot.

They walk, they were given eyes, hands, legs and some kind of capacity was given to them (electrical motor) which runs by a power source (battery) and with the power of that battery, they are able to move their hands, legs, so they walk, right?

So, we can say this is our creation. We created these small machines, and some functions are like the functions of the human being, at least that’s what scientists are trying and improve every day!

Easy part, right? Are you thinking why I am discussing this easy-to-understand-by-kids stuff with you in a kids\’ way? Just have a little patience and keep on focusing on me, while I am going to take you to my point.

Why would you give a TAIL or a BRAIN to a robot?

Now, if we decide to put a \”TAIL\” to robots, unlike human structure, what do you think we would do it for? (Some people I heard really have fun thinking that if we humans had a tail, as animals do) So that it can use its tail, right? So, if we set a \”tail\” in a robot, we will also program it for its purpose and for its functions so as to how and when and why it will use its tail, right?

Now, think the same for the brain. Though robotics is improving day by day, if we are capable of \’giving a brain to a robot\’, what do you think we would do it for? So that it can use its brain by itself to think freely without being programmed, right? But at least when we make a robot now, we set a program in its chips and program it for what purpose and when for which functions it will use it, right?

Imagine all the scientists of the world are trying to improve robotics, and we are waiting to see that one day they will be able to put brains into robots so that robots can use their brains freely on their own, that will be a scientific ultimate achievement and achievement of human capability, right? (Actually, improvements in robotics are happening as such) So, why would we do that? To set a robot for our use, isn\’t it, simple logic, simple understanding.
(This is a writing published in the year 2015. We are in 2025, now, we have AI, and we know how it works.)

Did you NOTICE, the CAPABILITY of all the ORGANS of OUR BODY is LIMITED, but ONE?

Now, think of us, human beings, our Creator has given us 100s of organs in our body and he sets the capability of those organs so they function accordingly to their capacity, right?
Now, what\’s the capacity of our eyes? Limited, isn\’t it?
What\’s the capacity of our hands, limited!
What\’s the capacity of our legs, veins, muscles, bones, etc.? Limited!
Just go by one after another organ of your body, all of those organs have got particular functions and all of them have got LIMITATIONS in their CAPABILITIES.

But the ONLY ORGAN in our body had NO LIMITATIONS as far as its capacity is concerned.
Do you understand what that ORGAN is?
You already know the answer, right?
It is our BRAIN. Huge, Enormous, Unlimited, isn\’t it!

See now, with our entire advancement and all-out achievement, we are putting in the effort to make a brain for a robot that could work for us on its own, but as for ourselves, our Creator already gave us that brain.

Now, USE your THOUGHTFUL MIND:

Leave all these theoretical ideas about scientists which does not affect you personally. I earnestly ask you to relate this to you personally. Remember, the more you can get close to the imagination, the more you can connect with something. So, think of yourself as a maker, give it some time, close your eyes and try to make something yourself and try to give every organ to it and have it work.

You will give each and every organ by keeping some expectation in your mind about that organ. So, if you are able to give a brain to that thing, you will have a lot of expectations out of that organ.

Now ask yourself, what will it be?
Obviously, it will be for your service.
So, think now, put yourself in a creator\’s shoes for the reason of understanding your creator better. Give that creation one by one, each organ and think why HE would give such an organ to HIS creation?

Why did your CREATOR give you that BRAIN?

Finally, here is the POINT that I tried to bring to you today.
I want you to THINK about this question.
Why the CREATOR you believe in gave us that enormous BRAIN?
Isn\’t HE gave that for HIS purpose?
Isn\’t HE created that organ for HIS purpose?
If our entire self is created by the Creator for HIS purpose and for HIS cause, isn\’t the brain also created to use FOR HIM?

Now look at you, look at us, what are we doing with that brain?
Think of a day of yours from opening your eyes from sleep to closing your eyes for sleeping, how do you use your brain, for what?
Did you use it for your creator?
Did you use it to THINK about HIM?
Simple question, simple thought!

That\’s the summary of this long talk, just to put this simple question.
If you believe you are a Creation of \’God\’,
If you believe \’God\’ has created you for HIS purpose,
Then your brain was also HE created for HIS purpose, to use that brain for HIM, to think about HIM.
Ask yourself, what are you doing with this brain?

How much of your time did you allocate for your Creator?

If you are a working person, student, homemaker, retired or doing whatever you do, busy going to work, or school, homework or doing household stuff, hanging out with family, friends, reading, having fun, TV, movies, games entertainment, travelling, camping, vacation, surfing net, watching youtube, eating, sleeping, toileting, bathing, what not! EVEN IF you are a very much a PRACTICING and RELIGIOUS person, ask yourself, of 24 hours a day, how many minutes you use your brain to THINK about your Creator, being a \’believer\’!

Maybe you are a good religious person, maybe you do your religious rituals, maybe you do your religious practice regularly, but do you really THINK about your CREATOR?

Now, if you do not understand what I meant by \”THINK about your creator\”, then let me make it simple to you. When you are newly married, you think about your spouse very often, even if you are busy at work, walking, transporting, eating, or before sleeping. Whatever you do, your loved ones come to your mind.

If you are a creative person, amidst your regular work, you THINK and create; if you are so much engrossed in work, you keep thinking about it while you are eating.

Do you understand now what I mean when I say \”Do you THINK about your creator?\”
Even if you are a person who does religious practices and worship regularly,
Apart from your regular worshipping practices, do you give it a THOUGHT about your creator?

Ask yourself this question.
You are a \’believer\’ in \’God\’ and that \’God\’ gave you the brain for HIS purpose, how are you using it for HIM? How many hours or even minutes do you THINK about your CREATOR?

What to THINK?

Maybe I made it too long, but I had a wide range of readers to cover, and did I make my point?
If you agree, then the question may arise, \”What do I think about my Creator?\”

Well, Nobody needs to tell you what to think. It\’s your job, just give your creator sometimes of your thoughts, you will see what happens!

But to give you a little to begin with, I will give you three aspects to know about.
Know about your body, yourself,
Know about the Earth, its surface, land and water and beneath,
Know about the space and the universe.
These are the most important elements that every human should learn.

Watch documentaries about space, the universe, the earth, the human body, the life of animals, insects, etc, about all the CREATIONS of your CREATOR and be amazed how your creator made these work, be amazed by his POWER and MIGHT, HIS GREATNESS, HIS PERFECTNESS in making those creations. Follow the scientist\’s explanations, but beware of the difference between scientific FACTS, scientific THEORIES and ASSUMPTIONS and the scientist\’s personal views. Even if you watched these before, now see them from a new perspective. The CREATOR that you believe in will BLOW your MIND.

But along the way, I might give you some very crucial points once you SET YOUR MIND for YOUR CREATOR.

And I ask Allah to guide you.
