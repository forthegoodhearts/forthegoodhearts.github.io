# Rule saw128: “Rasulullah Sallallahu A’laihi Wa Sallam” on the six sites

Owner's rule, 28 Oct 2026: in English, after “Rasulullah” always write “Sallallahu A’laihi Wa Sallam”.
This list shows, per article, what the free copy WOULD change. Nothing has been changed yet: the owner ticks per site in the PMS (Web J01).
The WordPress text is never changed; the free copy is corrected after every weekly rebuild (also in the page description Google shows).

- **Correct**: a salutation is already there but spelled differently (Sallillahu Alaihi WaSallam, Salla Allahu alaihi wa sallam, (SAW), ﷺ …) → “Sallallahu A’laihi Wa Sallam”.
- **Add**: nothing follows “Rasulullah” → “Sallallahu A’laihi Wa Sallam” is added (also inside quoted hadith, e.g. “O Rasulullah!”).
- **Left as written**: “Rasulullah’s …”, and “AsSalatu AsSalamu A’ala Rasulullah” (already a blessing).

Example: “That’s why Rasulullah sallillahu alaihi wa Sallam said …” → “That’s why Rasulullah Sallallahu A’laihi Wa Sallam said …”
